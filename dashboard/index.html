How a row earns its value

1 · Comparables, in three steps

Tokenized protocols reveal what the market pays per $1 of TVL, and it is not one price: in this snapshot the median runs . A row is priced by its own category's median when that category has three or more tokenized peers; by its category family's median when the category is thin but the family — categories running the same business — is not; and only then by the market-wide median. Every multiple names the peers behind it, below.

2 · A range, graded

Each row carries the range its peers' middle half implies — first to third quartile — and a confidence grade from the peer count and their spread. Medians and quartiles, never averages: one tokenized peer in this snapshot trades at 400× its TVL, and an average would let it price a category.

3 · Momentum

The 7-day TVL change, clamped to −40%…+80%, scales value by 0.76×–1.48×. Growing protocols are gaining the users a token would be sold to; shrinking ones are losing them.

4 · Longevity

Age scales value by 0.75×–1.25× over four years. A protocol that has run for years with no token has survived the audits, bear markets and exploits that killed younger rivals.
